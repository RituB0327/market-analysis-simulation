Market Intelligence Simulator is a web-based business market analysis tool designed to help entrepreneurs and students evaluate market opportunities using the TAM (Total Addressable Market), SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market), and SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market) framework.

The simulator allows users to enter product information, market area, potential customers, annual revenue per customer, and SAM/SOM percentages to calculate market size and visualize the opportunity. It also maintains a history of market analysis records and provides downloadable CSV and market reports.

The application also includes venture registration and login functionality, allowing users to create a venture with 2–3 team members. An admin dashboard provides access to registered users, venture information, team members, categories, cities, plans, and account status.

The project is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with browser localStorage used to maintain user and analysis data. It is designed as a lightweight market intelligence and business simulation prototype suitable for MBA projects, entrepreneurship programs, product management exercises, and startup market research.
